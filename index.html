About me

I grew up in Talas, Kyrgyzstan, and finished eleven years of school at Manas-Ata Lyceum. Several of those years were spent in a boarding-school environment, which taught me to manage my own time, adapt quickly, and rely on myself.

At school I served as Minister of Education in the student parliament, which gave me early practice in listening to people and turning ideas into action.

Today I'm studying Computer Science at Pacific States University in Los Angeles. I'm still early in my technical journey, and I like it that way: every new concept is something to take apart and understand. I'm especially interested in where technology meets business and creativity, which is why I also studied marketing through Ulush Education.
